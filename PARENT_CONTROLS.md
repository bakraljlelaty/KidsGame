# Parent Controls

A guide for parents and caregivers. Everything described here is on your device only — the app
has no accounts, no ads, no internet features, and collects nothing. See
[PRIVACY_CHECKLIST.md](PRIVACY_CHECKLIST.md).

## Opening the parent area (the "gate")

The parent area is protected by two barriers that are easy for you and effectively impossible for
a toddler:

1. **Hold both top corners of the screen at the same time for 3 seconds.** From the child's home
   screen, press and hold the top-left and top-right corners together. A small progress ring
   appears while you hold; releasing either corner cancels it.
2. **Enter the parent PIN** (4 digits) on the pad that appears.

The **default PIN is `2468`**. The app reminds you of this on the PIN screen until you change it —
please change it right away in **Settings → Change parent PIN** (you'll enter the current PIN,
then the new one twice). If you forget a changed PIN, the only reset is
*Settings → Delete all child data* — which also erases progress — so pick something you'll
remember.

## The dashboard sections

### Profile

- **Nickname** — a display nickname only; no real names are needed (or asked for).
- **Age group** — "Around 2 years" or "Around 3 years". An approximate group only, never a
  birth date.
- **Avatar** — a small picture the child sees on their home screen.
- **Language** — English or Arabic; changes both the app text and Milo's spoken voice.
- **Development stage** — how the games behave:
  - *Explorer*: one thing at a time, very large objects, quick help.
  - *Helper*: tap and drag, two or three choices, simple matching, small puzzles.
  - *Little Thinker*: up to five objects, four-piece puzzles, counting to three, little routines.
- **Adjust stage automatically** — when on, the app moves up a stage gently after several
  relaxed, hint-free play sessions. It is never based on speed, and you can always set the stage
  yourself.

### Games

- **Game access** — switch each of the six games on or off; only enabled games appear on the
  child's world map.
- **Play order** —
  - *Free choice*: your child picks any enabled game from the map.
  - *Guided order*: games open one after another in a gentle sequence.
- **Reset progress** — removes stars, stickers, and play history (after a confirmation). The
  profile and your settings are kept.

### Session

- **Session length** — 5, 10, 15, or 20 minutes, or *No timer*.
- **Daily limit** — a total cap of play time per calendar day (or no limit).
- **Break between sessions** — how long the app rests before a new session can start on its own.
- **Allow another session now** — your override: opens one extra session immediately, even
  during a break or after the daily limit.
- The section also shows roughly how long your child has played today.

**How a session ends:** a soft sound plays about a minute before time is up. Your child may
**always finish the mini-game they are playing** — the app never cuts an activity off. Afterwards
it returns to Milo, who is sleepy; the screen stays calm and quiet, and new sessions wait until
the break passes, a new day starts, or you allow one. This gives the day a gentle, predictable
ending instead of an abrupt stop.

### Progress

A relaxed look at how your child likes to play: approximate play time, games tried and finished,
how often hints appeared, which games are chosen most, which kinds of play came up (tapping,
matching, colours, routines…), and the current stage.

**What these numbers mean — and what they don't:** this is a *play overview, not an assessment*.
The app never grades, scores, or ranks your child, and nothing here diagnoses or measures
development. Hints appearing often just means the app offered help; children this age vary
enormously and all of it is normal. Use the page for conversation ("you fed the animals
today!"), not evaluation.

### Settings

- **Music / Sound effects / Voice instructions** — three independent audio switches.
- **App language** — English or Arabic (the parent screens follow the language's reading
  direction; game scenes stay as they are).
- **Reduce motion** — calmer animations and no particle effects.
- **High contrast** — stronger outlines and colours for interactive objects.
- **Gentle vibration** — soft haptic feedback on success (off = none at all).
- **Left-handed layout** — moves helper buttons in games to the left side (and Milo to the
  right) so a left hand doesn't cover them.
- **Restore default settings** — resets the above (your PIN is deliberately kept).
- **Change parent PIN** — see above.
- **Delete all child data** — permanently removes the profile, settings, progress, stars,
  stickers, and session history from this device, after a confirmation. Nothing is stored
  anywhere else, so this is a complete erase.
